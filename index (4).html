About

I'm an independent security researcher and penetration tester focused on offensive security — web applications, APIs, and network-layer attack surfaces. I find real vulnerabilities in production systems and report them responsibly. My target environments span government agencies, defense contractors, EU institutions, healthcare infrastructure, financial platforms, and consumer tech.

The disclosure portfolio covers 40+ organizations with formal recognition from NASA, NCIIPC (Government of India), CERT-EU, Thales Group, Samsung, Electronic Arts, and others. The breadth is deliberate: adapting methodology to a NASA VDP, a pharma CVD program, and a fintech bug bounty requires reading different threat models and scoping constraints — not just running the same tool chain everywhere.

I've completed two penetration testing internships, hold the CAP and CNSP from The SecOps Group, and supplement practical work with CTF competition to sharpen fundamentals. I'm a final-year B.Tech student at LNCT Bhopal (graduating 2027), currently pursuing OSCP and targeting full-time penetration testing roles — client-facing VAPT, red team support, or product security at a company with a real attack surface.
